Quick answers

The things people ask before their first order.

How does same-day pickup work?

Choose Collect from shop at checkout. The shop pulls your pair, checks it and messages you when it's ready — usually 20–60 minutes. Bring your order number and a photo ID.

What if the size is wrong?

Every shop offers a 30-day exchange on unworn pairs in the original box. Walk in with your receipt for the quickest swap, or contact us and we'll arrange a pickup.

Are the shoes genuine?

Yes. Every pair is stock held by a named shop, inspected before dispatch. We don't run a warehouse or a drop-ship operation.

Can I return a sale item?

Sale items are exchange-only, unless they arrive damaged or incorrect. Fit advice is always free — ask before you buy if you're between sizes.

Do you deliver outside Bengaluru?

Yes, by courier in 2–5 working days. Pickup and same-day delivery are limited to serviceable Bengaluru pincodes.

Prefer a specific shop?

Each shop's phone, address and opening hours are on its profile. Call them directly — they won't mind.
